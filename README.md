## The line

> You left it on my couch with your name on it, and in dog law possession is nine-tenths of lunch.

**96 characters** (limit: 1–200).

## Why it fits the brief

- **Dog's own voice, caught red-pawed:** first person, answering for itself right after being caught — no narrator, no setup.
- **Standalone:** the scene (caught eating the homework) is inside the line itself — it needs no setup to land.
- **The laugh is a twist on dog logic:** the dog isn't sorry — it cites "dog law" and bends the old saying "possession is nine-tenths of the law" into "nine-tenths of lunch". Under the dog's legal system the homework wasn't stolen; it was already lunch, and the human's name on it was just a label.
- **Real dog priorities:** couch, territory ("my couch"), food. No human embarrassment, no apology, no "good boy" routine.
- **Fresh:** no cat to blame, no "it tasted good", no begging for forgiveness. The joke is the dog's confident legal defense.

## How to run

```bash
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./out python3 main.py
cat out/entry.txt
```

`main.py` (pure Python stdlib, no network) reads `entry.txt` from beside itself, strips at most one trailing newline, and writes the line plus one trailing newline to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), mode `0644`.
